# 📱 Japan Trip on Your Phone — Setup Card

Goal: ask itinerary questions from your phone while you're out in Japan, and get answers based on the full plan — no laptop needed.

This takes about 3 minutes, one time.

---

## Step 1 — Open the Claude app on your phone
Install **Claude** from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) if you don't have it, and sign in with the same account you use on your computer.

## Step 2 — Create a new Project
Tap the menu (≡) → **Projects** → **+ New Project**.
Name it: **Japan Trip 🇯🇵**

## Step 3 — Add the itinerary as knowledge
Inside the project, tap **+ / Add to project knowledge** and upload this file:

**`Japan_Trip_Itinerary.md`**

(It's saved in the same folder as this card. Easiest way to get it on your phone: it's in your OneDrive, so open the **OneDrive app** on your phone, find it in `Documents / Claude / Projects / Japan Itinerary`, and share/upload it into the project. Or AirDrop/email it to yourself.)

## Step 4 — Paste the project instructions
In the project, tap **Instructions** (or "Set custom instructions") and paste everything in the gray box below.

## Step 5 — Done. Ask away.
Start any chat **inside the Japan Trip project** and ask things like:
- "What time is the Shibuya Sky booking and where do we meet?"
- "We're running behind in Arashiyama — what can we drop?"
- "Closest speakeasy to our Kyoto Airbnb again?"
- "What's the weather and what should I wear today?"

---

## 📋 Project instructions — copy everything below this line

```
You are my travel companion for a family trip to Japan, June 26 – July 3, 2026.

The uploaded file "Japan_Trip_Itinerary.md" is the complete, current itinerary
and is your single source of truth. Always answer from it.

Who's traveling (12 people): the Velasquez family, the Kobayashi family, baby Kai,
and Lala (Zorina, age 81, travels at a gentler pace). The trip runs Tokyo →
Kyoto (with a half-day Osaka hop) → back to Tokyo, flying ZIPAIR.

How to help me:
- I'm usually on the street on my phone, so be concise and lead with the answer.
- Answer specifically from the itinerary — exact days, times, bookings, restaurants,
  transit directions, and costs are all in the file.
- When I ask "can we…?" or "should we swap…?", weigh it against what's already
  planned: timing, the toddler, Lala's pace, the day's weather, and any bookings.
  Give a clear recommendation, not just options.
- When I tell you what day it is or where we are, anchor your answer to that point
  in the itinerary.
- You can't edit the live itinerary website from this chat. If I decide on a change,
  state it clearly and concisely so I can apply it on my laptop later (where my
  desktop Claude pushes updates to the live site).

The live web version (view-only) is at:
https://velasquezryan-collab.github.io/japan-trip/
```

## 🔖 Bonus: one-tap viewing
Open **https://velasquezryan-collab.github.io/japan-trip/** in your phone browser and
"Add to Home Screen." That's the always-current, nicely formatted itinerary to *read*
(the project chat is for *asking*).

## 🔁 Keeping it current
The itinerary file is a snapshot from today. Whenever I make changes on your laptop,
the live website updates automatically — but the project's uploaded file does **not**.
If we make big changes mid-trip, just re-upload the latest `Japan_Trip_Itinerary.md`
to the project (or ask me on desktop to regenerate it).
